## Usage

Field group lays out related editors and joins their borders. It owns only the layout, the seams and the group name: each editor keeps its own value, `name`, label, state and validation, and the group submits nothing itself. `label` names the tuple ("Canvas size"); every editor still needs its own complete `label`, because prefixes like `W` are visual shorthand.

To stack separate Fields with spacing, use Field's `group` slot instead.

### Tuples

Editors share the main axis equally. Keep a related value that is not part of the tuple, such as opacity, outside the group so it reads as separate.

### Mixed members

Buttons and Toggles can join beside the editors, for example a lock between width and height. A Separator divides editors and takes the perpendicular orientation automatically.

### Member states

Each editor keeps its own state inside the group. A read-only or disabled editor shows its usual treatment while its neighbours stay editable.

<Preview
  title="Read-only and disabled"
  align="stretch"
  code={`<tp-field-group label="Retry policy" style="max-inline-size:24rem">
  <tp-input-group><tp-input label="Timeout" default-value="30" readonly></tp-input><span slot="suffix">s</span></tp-input-group>
  <tp-input-group><tp-input label="Retries" default-value="3"></tp-input></tp-input-group>
  <tp-input-group><tp-input label="Backoff" default-value="2" disabled></tp-input><span slot="suffix">×</span></tp-input-group>
</tp-field-group>`}
/>

### Orientation

`orientation="vertical"` stacks the editors, stretches them to the widest one and joins their block edges. Use it for long tuples that would become unreadable in one row.

<Preview
  title="Vertical"
  align="stretch"
  code={`<tp-field-group label="Shipping address" orientation="vertical" style="max-inline-size:20rem">
  <tp-input label="Street address" placeholder="Street address" default-value="221 Market Street"></tp-input>
  <tp-input label="Apartment, suite" placeholder="Apartment, suite (optional)"></tp-input>
  <tp-input label="City" placeholder="City" default-value="San Francisco"></tp-input>
</tp-field-group>`}
/>

### Separate editors

Set the `joined` property to `false` to keep every editor's own corners with the standard gap, while still grouping and naming them.
