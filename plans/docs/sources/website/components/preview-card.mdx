## Usage

A Preview Card shows nonessential context about its subject, like a profile behind a mention or the summary behind a link. Hover or keyboard focus opens it after `open-delay`; pressing the trigger still follows the link. It never traps focus or blocks the page, so keep anything needed to finish a task in a Popover instead.

### Delays

`open-delay` (600ms by default) avoids flashing cards while the pointer passes over, and `close-delay` (300ms) gives the pointer time to travel into the card. Shorten them when previews are the point of the surface.

### Placement and arrow

`placement` sets the preferred side and alignment, which flip on collision. `show-arrow` points the card at its subject.

### Pointer entry

By default the pointer can move into the card to select or scroll its content. `disable-hoverable-popup` closes the card as soon as the pointer leaves the trigger, for purely glanceable previews.
