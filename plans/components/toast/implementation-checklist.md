### Resolved implementation design

- ToastManager owns immutable ordered snapshots, atomic/reentrant publications, timers, causes, promise settlement generations and lifecycle metadata. ToastProvider is the non-rendered policy/environment binding; TpToast delegates every queue operation to it.
- Source callbacks onClose/onRemove use timeout, close-action, action, swipe, programmatic, anchor-removed or provider-destroyed; these are lifecycle causes, never ChangeReason.
- Default native manual-popover viewport shares the established anchored-layer policy and keeps shadow parts/themes. Optional explicit container relocates only the viewport into an owned isolated shadow layer, with structural stylesheet, owner-token inheritance on its wrapper, terminal viewport style hooks preserved, and external registered presentation parts.
