Border (`tp-mo-border`) opens the Motion › Surface group. It replaces the border of the surface it wraps, such as a Card, Button, Input or Select, with a drawn one at the same place, radius and width, and the surface keeps its size, focus ring and behavior. The border either glows or is dashed. The glow colors the line from a list of colors spread around it, turning white-hot where its light is closest, with a bloom and an optional wash. Its light follows the pointer, a point or a moving element, or the glow runs on its own as evenly spaced comets, a turning sweep, a breathing pulse or a passing glint. Dashes march, breathe or draw the border in, on entering the view, on hover or focus, or scrubbed by a Scroll trigger. One `trigger` (`none`, `always`, `hover`, `focus`, `pointer`) decides when it shows, and fades take a duration, an easing and a delay before fading out. Segment children (`tp-mo-border-segment`) replace the line with pieces that move through their own states on hover, focus or in view, each with its own timing, an optional exit path, and a plain or glowing look.
