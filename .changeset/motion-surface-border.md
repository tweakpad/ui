Glow border (`tp-mo-glow-border`) and Segment border (`tp-mo-segment-border`) open the Motion › Surface group. Each replaces the border of the surface it wraps, such as a Card, Button, Input or Select, with a drawn one at the same place, radius and width, and the surface keeps its size and behavior while the border draws its focus ring and invalid state. Glow border colors the line from a list of colors spread around it, turning white-hot where its light is closest, with a bloom and an optional wash; its light follows the pointer, a point or a moving element, or the glow runs on its own as evenly spaced comets, a turning sweep, a breathing pulse or a passing glint, and fades take a duration, an easing and a delay before fading out. Segment border draws its `tp-mo-border-segment` children over the line: pieces that move through their own states, measured along the line or named as corners and edge middles, on hover, on focus or once in view, each with its own timing and an optional exit path, as a plain line or a glow. One `trigger` decides when each plays.
