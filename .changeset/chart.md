`tp-data-visualization` is now `tp-chart` (`TpChart`, the `Chart` framework bindings, `Chart…` types and `chart-…` parts), and it draws charts itself. `type` draws lines, bars, lollipops, pies and donuts, radar shapes or radial rings from `data` and keyed `series`, lines and bars on category, number or time axes (`x-axis`, `y-axis`, `x-domain`, `y-domain`), with dashed grid lines, a line at zero, bars that hang below it, and `stacked` bars. Inspection shows a band behind bars or a crosshair over lines, with every series in the tooltip, by pointer and with the arrow keys. A series takes `negativeColor` for its values below zero and `format` for how its values read; `legendOptions.value` adds each series' `latest` value or `sum` to the legend, and `legendOptions = false` hides it. `loading` shows placeholder marks in the Skeleton's shimmer. Pie, radar and radial marks take `angle-range` (a half circle is `-90 90`) and `inner-radius`, and the `center` slot places a total in their middle. The legend can stand in a column at the `start` or `end` of the plot under a `title`, or `inline`, labeling radial rings and line ends on the marks. A `renderer` of your own still draws any other chart in the same frame. Plot and Chart share their axis options, ticks and labels.
