## Usage

Wrap one surface in Segment border, a Card, a Button, an Input, a Text area, a Select, an Input group, a Toggle or any element with a border, and add `tp-mo-border-segment` children. Segment border hides the surface's border and draws its own line at exactly the same place, radius and width, so nothing around it moves, then draws the segments over it. It also draws the focus ring and shows an invalid control in the destructive color. The drawing lets pointer events through and is hidden from assistive technology.

Each segment is a stretch of the border from a start to an end, measured clockwise from its `origin`, in percent of the border's length or in pixels. `draw` lists the states it moves through while the border is hovered, and it plays back when the pointer leaves. Above, one segment starts at the top center, `origin="top"`, with no length, and grows both ways to `-50% 50%`: it runs along the top, down both sides and closes at the bottom center. Values below 0% or past 100% wrap through the origin.

Each segment has its own `duration`, `delay` and `easing`; the steps between its states share the duration. Plain segments are lines in the theme's primary color, changed with `--tp-mo-border-segment-color`, `--tp-mo-border-segment-width` and `--tp-mo-border-segment-cap` on a segment or on the border. Under reduced motion the segments jump to their last state and back.

### Patterns

Where segments start and which way they grow make the pattern. A position can also name a point of the box: a corner, `top-left`, `top-right`, `bottom-right` or `bottom-left`, or the middle of an edge, `top`, `right`, `bottom` or `left`. Points keep lines meeting exactly at the corners whatever the surface's proportions. A point is reached the short way round from the segment's previous state, and a stretch that ends at a point runs clockwise to it.

Each of these Buttons draws the same full border on hover: one segment traced all the way round from a corner, two running edge by edge from opposite corners, two spreading both ways from opposite corners, and four growing out from the middle of each edge.

### Leaving

`leave` gives a segment states to run from where it is when the pointer leaves, before it rests; without it, the segment plays back. Here each line enters its edge at one corner on hover and, on leaving, runs on out through the other corner instead of retracting, so the lines keep turning the same way: across the top and bottom, up and down the sides, or all the way round.

### Drawing the border in

`trigger="always"` plays the segments once the border is in view and holds their last state. With `--tp-mo-segment-border-color: transparent` the line under the segments is gone, so a segment in the border color that grows to the whole border traces it in. A short segment with `glow`, drawn as light in `--tp-mo-segment-border-glow-colors`, leads the trace on the same timing and settles into the corner.

### Focus

`trigger="focus"` plays the segments while focus is inside the surface. The segments are then the focus indicator, so the border draws no separate focus ring; give them the ring color and a width that stands out. `trigger="none"` keeps them at their first state.

<Preview
  title="Focus"
  code={`<tp-field label="Call sign" style="inline-size:min(100%, 20rem)">
  <tp-mo-segment-border trigger="focus" style="--tp-mo-border-segment-color:var(--tp-ring);--tp-mo-border-segment-width:2px">
    <tp-input placeholder="Focus to draw the border"></tp-input>
    <tp-mo-border-segment origin="bottom" draw="0% 0%, -50% 50%" duration="400" easing="ease-out"></tp-mo-border-segment>
  </tp-mo-segment-border>
</tp-field>`}

### Loops

`repeat` loops a segment, a number of times or `infinite`, and `alternate` plays every other run backwards. A short glowing segment looping around a search field shows that a search is running.

<Preview
  title="Scanner"
  code={`<tp-mo-segment-border trigger="always" style="inline-size:min(100%, 22rem)">
  <tp-input type="search" label="Search the logbook" placeholder="Vessel, berth or date"></tp-input>
  <tp-mo-border-segment glow repeat="infinite" draw="0% 12%, 100% 112%" duration="2400" easing="linear"></tp-mo-border-segment>
</tp-mo-segment-border>`}
/>

### Limits

- Segment border draws over one surface, its first child element other than a segment.
- The light of glowing segments reaches outside the surface, so an ancestor that clips its overflow cuts it off.
- Segments play from their trigger, not from the scroll position.
