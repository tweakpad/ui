## Usage

The editor fills its box, `20rem` tall by default, and opens with the whole image in view. The wheel zooms toward the pointer, a sideways swipe pans, and dragging pans. On touch, one finger pans and two fingers pinch. A zoom of `1` shows the image at its natural size, so `actual` in the controls or the <Keys keys="1" /> key shows every pixel.

`fit` sets how small the image may get. `contain` stops when the whole image fits, `cover` while it still fills the frame, and `none` lets it shrink further, down to `min-zoom`: a zoom such as `0.25`, or a length such as `8rem` for the image's longer side. Panning keeps to the same rule, so a covering image never shows a gap.

### Avatars

A crop region frames the part of the image to keep and dims the rest. `crop="circle"` with `fit="cover"` is the familiar profile-photo picker: the circle stays put, the image moves and zooms under it, and it always fills the circle.

The value is the frame in image space: `{ x, y, width, height, rotation }` in the image's natural pixels. Store it to reopen the editor where it was, or send it with the original file to crop on a server. `toBlob()` cuts the region in the browser, at natural resolution or scaled down with `maxSize`; a circle exports its square.

<Code
  language="typescript"
  code={`editor.addEventListener('tp-value-commit', (event) => save(event.detail.value));
const blob = await editor.toBlob({ type: 'image/webp', quality: 0.9, maxSize: 512 });`}
/>

### Adjustable region

`region-mode="adjustable"` adds handles, so people choose both the part and its size. A drag inside the region moves it and a drag outside pans the image; `region-drag="pan"` makes every drag pan the image and leaves the region to its handles. `aspect-ratio` holds the region's shape while it resizes, and without one, Shift keeps the current shape. `region-min-width` and its siblings bound the size, as lengths or percentages of the viewport, and `guides` shows thirds while it changes.

### Rotation and controls

`controls` lists the bar's controls in order: zoom, rotate, straighten, fit, actual size and reset. Rotation turns the image about the frame's centre, and with `cover` the zoom rises as needed to keep the frame filled. `pinch-rotate` lets two fingers turn the image as they pinch. A transparent image shows the checker behind it, sized and colored with `--tp-image-editor-checker-size` and `--tp-image-editor-checker-color`.

### In a scrolling page

On a page that scrolls, an editor that takes every wheel and touch traps the reader. `cooperative-gestures` gives plain wheels and single-finger drags back to the page; Control or Meta with the wheel, a trackpad pinch or two fingers still zoom and pan the image. `wheel="pan"` makes the wheel pan instead of zoom.

### Keyboard

The image is one tab stop, and an adjustable region is a second one.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| <Keys keys="ArrowLeft" />, <Keys keys="ArrowRight" />, <Keys keys="ArrowUp" />, <Keys keys="ArrowDown" /> | Pan the image; with Shift in larger steps, with Alt in smaller ones |
| <Keys keys="+" />, <Keys keys="-" /> | Zoom in or out |
| <Keys keys="0" />, <Keys keys="1" /> | Fit the image, or show it at its natural size |
| <Keys keys="Home" />, <Keys keys="End" /> | Zoom to the smallest or largest size |
| <Keys keys="[" />, <Keys keys="]" /> | Rotate a quarter turn left or right |
| <Keys keys="shift+[" />, <Keys keys="shift+]" /> | Rotate a degree left or right |
| Arrow keys on the region | Move the region; with Shift in larger steps |
| <Keys keys="alt+ArrowRight" />, <Keys keys="alt+ArrowDown" /> and their opposites | Resize the region from its end edge |
| <Keys keys="Escape" /> | While moving or resizing the region, put it back |
