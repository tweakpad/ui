## Usage

An Aspect-ratio box takes its width from its container and derives its height from `ratio`, width divided by height: `1.7778` for 16:9, `1` for a square. The space is reserved before the media loads, so feeds, galleries and video embeds do not jump, and the content can never stretch the box. Always set the ratio. The box adds no image semantics, so the image keeps its own `alt`.

One 16:9 box keeps its shape in a sidebar, a card and a feature slot. The ratio is its only size input: give it a width, or let the container shrink it, and the height follows.

### Product photos

<Preview
  code={`<div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:repeat(2,minmax(0,12rem));gap:var(--tp-space-4)">
  <tp-aspect-ratio ratio="1" fit="cover" style="border-radius:var(--tp-radius-lg);background:var(--tp-muted)">
    <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1501785888041-af3ef285b470?w=800&q=80" alt="Mountain lake at dawn, cropped to fill">
  </tp-aspect-ratio>
  <tp-aspect-ratio ratio="1" fit="contain" style="border-radius:var(--tp-radius-lg);background:var(--tp-muted)">
    <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1501785888041-af3ef285b470?w=800&q=80" alt="Mountain lake at dawn, shown whole">
  </tp-aspect-ratio>
</div>`}
/>

`fit` sets how an image or a video fills the box: cropped to fill it for a uniform grid of thumbnails, or shown whole inside it, with the box's background around it, when nothing may be cut off. It never changes the ratio.
