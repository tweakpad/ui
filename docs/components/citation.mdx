## Usage

A Citation sits in running text after the statement it supports: in an article, a report or a generated answer. Its label names the source, and with an `href` it links to it. Use one style per passage: labelled chips like these, or numbered markers that point to a Citation list. The default outline treatment reads below the surrounding copy; `variant` gives a citation more or less weight when it needs it.

  script={`import { fileTextIcon } from '@tweakpad/ui/icons/file-text';

/>

`src` shows the site's icon and keeps its space while it loads; without it, or when it fails, the `icon` slot is used, else a generic web mark. A long label ends in an ellipsis. A link to another site opens in a new tab unless `target` says otherwise, and while it is hovered or focused an external-link mark fades in over the end of the label without changing its width, so the text around it never moves. A source with no address, such as an interview, is plain text and takes no tab stop.

### Previewing a source

<Preview
  align="stretch"
  code={`<p style="max-inline-size:40rem;margin:0;line-height:1.75">
  Turbine blades now last about twenty years underwater
  <tp-citation
    href="https://energy.example/tidal"
    src="data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg' viewBox='0 0 16 16'%3E%3Crect width='16' height='16' rx='4' fill='%2316a34a'/%3E%3C/svg%3E"
    source-title="Blade wear in fast tidal streams"
    description="Ten-year inspection results from three test sites, with corrosion and fatigue measurements."
  >energy.example</tp-citation>.
</p>`}
/>

A `description` adds a card that opens after a short hover or on keyboard focus, with the site's icon, its host, `source-title` and the description; the `preview` slot adds content of your own. Use `source-title` for the full title when the label is only the site's name. The card is supplementary: the citation still opens its link, and the destination should carry everything a reader needs.

A Citation list numbers its entries in document order. A marker in the text names an entry by its `id` in `source` and takes the entry's number, address, title and preview, so an empty marker renders as a plain number, raised like a footnote marker by `superscript`. Give each marker its entry's fragment as `href`: activating it moves focus to the entry, and the entry opens the site. Editing an entry updates every marker that names it.

A horizontal list wraps its entries in rows, a compact strip of numbered sources under a generated answer. `start` and `index` change the numbering when a strip continues an earlier one.
