## Usage

Color alone never carries the meaning, so give every dot a `label` unless nearby text already states the status. The label is shown beside the dot with `show-label`, or kept as the dot's accessible name and shown in a tooltip. The label is exposed once: as text when it is shown, otherwise as the name of the dot, which has an image role. The tooltip repeats that name visually and adds no description.

### Variants

`variant` picks the color from the theme's status roles: `default` for active or informational states, `secondary` for neutral ones such as paused or offline, and `success`, `warning` and `destructive` for healthy, degraded and failed.

For any other color, set `--tp-status-dot-color` on the dot or an ancestor; `--tp-status-dot-size` sets its diameter.

### Label beside the dot

With `show-label`, the label renders as text after the dot. It takes the font and color of its context and wraps when space runs out, with the dot centered on its first line. Here each status is the supporting text of a list row.

### Label in a tooltip

Without `show-label`, the dot stands alone: the label becomes its accessible name and appears in a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus, so the dot joins the tab order. Hover a dot, or press Tab to reach it. Set `tooltip="false"` for dense layouts where the status is also stated elsewhere: the label stays the name, without a tooltip or a tab stop. Escape closes the tooltip and keeps focus.

When `show-label` is turned on while the dot has focus, the dot keeps focus until it is left, then leaves the tab order.

A dot with an empty `label` is decorative: it is hidden from assistive technology and never focusable.

### Motion

`motion="pulse"` fades the dot out and in; `motion="ping"` sends a ring out of it. Neither moves the dot, its label or the content around it, and both rest under reduced motion. Keep motion for live or changing states, so it keeps its meaning. Under reduced motion (`motion-policy="reduce"` on the dot or an ancestor, or the `prefers-reduced-motion` media feature) the dot rests without a ring.

Status dot adds no live region. Wrap dots whose changes must be announced in an element with `role="status"`.
