## Usage

A Status dot marks the state of something at a glance: a service, a teammate, a build. Color alone never carries the meaning, so give every dot a `label` unless nearby text already states the status; the label shows beside the dot, or stays the dot's name and appears in a tooltip. A dot with an empty label is decorative, hidden from assistive technology and never focusable. A Status dot adds no live region: put dots whose changes must be announced inside an element with `role="status"`.

/>

With `show-label`, the label is text after the dot: it takes the font and color of its context and wraps when space runs out, with the dot centered on its first line. Here each status is the supporting line of a List row.

In a compact column the dot stands alone: its label becomes its accessible name and appears in a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus, so the dot joins the tab order. Where the status is also stated elsewhere, turning the tooltip off keeps the name without the tooltip or the tab stop.

### Recording indicator

<Preview
  code={`<div style="display:flex;flex-wrap:wrap;align-items:center;gap:var(--tp-space-6)">
  <tp-status-dot variant="destructive" label="Recording" show-label motion="pulse"></tp-status-dot>
  <tp-status-dot variant="success" label="Live" show-label motion="ping"></tp-status-dot>
  <tp-status-dot variant="secondary" label="Idle" show-label></tp-status-dot>
</div>`}
/>

A pulse fades the dot out and in; a ping sends a ring out of it. Neither moves the dot, its label or anything around it, and both rest under reduced motion. Keep motion for live or changing states, so it keeps its meaning.

### Variants

<Preview
  code={`<div style="display:flex;flex-wrap:wrap;align-items:center;gap:var(--tp-space-6)">
  <tp-status-dot label="Syncing" show-label></tp-status-dot>
  <tp-status-dot variant="secondary" label="Paused" show-label></tp-status-dot>
  <tp-status-dot variant="success" label="Healthy" show-label></tp-status-dot>
  <tp-status-dot variant="warning" label="Delayed" show-label></tp-status-dot>
  <tp-status-dot variant="destructive" label="Failed" show-label></tp-status-dot>
</div>`}
/>

`variant` picks the color from the theme's status roles: the default for active or informational states, `secondary` for neutral ones such as paused or offline, and `success`, `warning` and `destructive` for healthy, degraded and failed. For any other color, set `--tp-status-dot-color` on the dot or an ancestor.
