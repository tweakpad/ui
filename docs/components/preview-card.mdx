## Usage

A Preview card shows context people can do without, like the profile behind a mention or the summary behind a link. Hover or keyboard focus opens it after a delay, and pressing the trigger still follows the link. It never traps focus or blocks the page, so anything needed to finish a task belongs in a Popover, and a short description of a control in a Tooltip.

### Release notes

<Preview
  code={`<tp-preview-card label="Release notes" open-delay="150" close-delay="100">
  <tp-button slot="trigger" variant="link" href="#release-2-4">Release 2.4</tp-button>
  <div style="display:grid;gap:var(--tp-space-2);max-inline-size:18rem">
    <div style="display:flex;align-items:center;gap:var(--tp-space-2)">
      <strong>Release 2.4</strong>
      <tp-badge variant="secondary">Latest</tp-badge>
    </div>
    <span style="color:var(--tp-muted-foreground)">Shared workspaces, faster search indexing and 38 bug fixes.</span>
  </div>
</tp-preview-card>`}
/>

The open delay keeps cards from flashing as the pointer passes over a page full of links, and the close delay gives the pointer time to travel into the card. On a changelog, where previews are the point, `open-delay` and `close-delay` shorten both.

### Repository links

<Preview
  code={`<tp-preview-card label="Repository summary" placement="inline-end center" show-arrow>
  <tp-button slot="trigger" variant="link" href="#tweakpad-ui">tweakpad/ui</tp-button>
  <div style="display:grid;gap:var(--tp-space-2);max-inline-size:16rem">
    <strong>tweakpad/ui</strong>
    <span style="color:var(--tp-muted-foreground)">Accessible web components with a shared theme.</span>
    <span style="font-size:var(--tp-text-xs);color:var(--tp-muted-foreground)">TypeScript · 2.1k stars · Updated today</span>
  </div>
</tp-preview-card>`}
/>

In a list of links, a card beside the link keeps the next rows visible. `placement` sets the preferred side and alignment, which flip near the viewport edge, and `show-arrow` points the card at its subject.

### Invoice references

<Preview
  code={`<tp-preview-card label="Invoice INV-2041" disable-hoverable-popup>
  <tp-button slot="trigger" variant="link" href="#inv-2041">INV-2041</tp-button>
  <div style="display:grid;gap:var(--tp-space-1)">
    <strong>$4,820.00</strong>
    <span style="color:var(--tp-muted-foreground)">Due Nov 1 · Northwind Traders</span>
  </div>
</tp-preview-card>`}
/>

The pointer can usually move into the card to select or scroll its content. A figure people only glance at needs none of that: `disable-hoverable-popup` closes the card as soon as the pointer leaves the trigger.
