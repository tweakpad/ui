## Usage

A Card groups content that belongs to one subject: a form, a plan, a metric. It lays out the header, the content and the footer, and everything placed in them stays yours. Put a real heading in the `header` slot so the card joins the document outline. A Card has no click target of its own: when it should lead somewhere, put a link on its heading or a Button in its footer, so the interactive element has its own name and focus.

### Settings section

<Preview
  code={`<tp-card style="max-inline-size:32rem">
  <h2 slot="header">Billing</h2>
  <p slot="description">Pro plan, renews on November 1, 2026</p>
  <tp-button slot="action" variant="outline" size="sm">Manage</tp-button>
  <p>12 of 15 seats in use. Seats added mid-cycle are prorated on the next invoice.</p>
  <p slot="footer" style="font-size:var(--tp-text-sm);color:var(--tp-muted-foreground)">Last invoice paid on October 1</p>
</tp-card>`}
/>

The `action` slot puts a control beside the title, for the one thing a reader does with the whole card; the footer then holds metadata instead of actions. Every section is optional and an empty one takes no space, so a card with only content is a padded surface for a short note.

The small size tightens the padding for grids, sidebars and cards inside other surfaces. With section colors off, the sections share one fill, which keeps a row of figures quiet; with borders off, fills alone separate the sections, for a card on a contrasting background.

### Floating notice

<Preview
  code={`<tp-card elevated style="max-inline-size:24rem">
  <h2 slot="header">Storage almost full</h2>
  <p slot="description">9.2 GB of 10 GB used</p>
  <p>Upgrade to keep syncing new files and version history.</p>
  <tp-button slot="footer" variant="ghost" size="sm">Dismiss</tp-button>
  <tp-button slot="footer" size="sm">Upgrade</tp-button>
</tp-card>`}
/>

`elevated` adds a shadow and nothing else. Keep it for a card that sits above the page, such as a notice pinned over content, not for every card in a grid.
