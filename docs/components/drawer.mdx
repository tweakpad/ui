## Usage

A Drawer is a Dialog attached to an edge of the screen. It has the same slots, focus management, dismissal and backdrop, and adds the edge, swipe gestures and snap points. Swiping toward its edge closes it; the trigger and the close action keep working for people who do not swipe. Reach for it on small screens, where a sheet from the bottom is easier to reach than a centered window, and for side panels that keep the page in view.

Around the drawers, Drawer Provider and Drawer Indent let the page recede behind an open sheet, and Drawer Virtual Keyboard Provider keeps the focused field above the on-screen keyboard.

Task details open beside the list they belong to, and a workspace switcher from the start of the page. `edge` is logical, so `inline-end` is the right side of a left-to-right page and the left of a right-to-left one. A desktop side panel turns gestures off with `swipe-enabled="false"`, so a stray drag over its fields does nothing.

### Nearby places on a map

<Preview
  code={`<tp-drawer id="drawer-places" label="Nearby places" description="12 results within 2 km." show-swipe-handle>
  <tp-button slot="trigger" variant="outline">Explore nearby</tp-button>
  <p style="margin:0">Drag the handle to expand the list, or release below the lowest point to close.</p>
  <tp-button slot="close" variant="outline">Done</tp-button>
</tp-drawer>`}
  script={`const drawer = document.getElementById('drawer-places');
drawer.snapPoints = [0.25, 0.5, 1];
drawer.defaultSnapPoint = 0.5;`}
/>

A results sheet over a map rests at partial heights, so readers see the map and the list together and drag for more. `snapPoints` lists the heights; with `show-swipe-handle` the handle is also a slider the arrow keys move. Each move is proposed with a cancelable `tp-snap-point-change`, and `setSnapPoint()` moves the sheet from code.

A Drawer inside another drawer's body opens a follow-up step on top of it. The parent stays open and recedes behind the new sheet; closing the child returns to the parent where the reader left it.

### Required acknowledgement

<Preview
  code={`<tp-drawer id="drawer-terms" label="Updated terms of service" description="Effective November 1, 2026.">
  <tp-button slot="trigger" variant="outline">Review terms</tp-button>
  <p style="margin:0">We changed how workspace data is retained after an account is closed. Review the summary before you continue.</p>

</tp-drawer>`}
  script={`document.getElementById('drawer-terms').dismissible = false;`}

With the `dismissible` property set to `false`, outside presses, Escape and swipes leave the drawer open, and only the close action ends it.

In a chat app, a swipe from the screen edge opens the channel list. A Drawer Swipe Area in the `swipe-area` slot is that strip: size and place it where the swipe should start, and keep the trigger for everyone who does not swipe.

## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| <Keys keys="esc" /> | Proposes closing the drawer, unless it is not dismissible. |
| <Keys keys="up" /> <Keys keys="down" /> | On the swipe handle of a bottom sheet with snap points, expands to the next snap point or shrinks to the previous one; a side drawer uses the left and right arrows. |
| <Keys keys="home" /> <Keys keys="end" /> | On the swipe handle, moves to the lowest or highest snap point. |
